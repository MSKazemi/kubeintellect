### Proposed Fix:
Create a Role and RoleBinding in the `scenario-test` namespace to grant the `limited-sa` service account the required permissions to list pods.

### Fix Command:
```yaml
apiVersion: rbac.authorization.k8s.io/v1
kind: Role
metadata:
  namespace: scenario-test
  name: pod-reader
rules:
- apiGroups: [""]
  resources: ["pods"]
  verbs: ["get", "list"]
---
apiVersion: rbac.authorization.k8s.io/v1
kind: RoleBinding
metadata:
  namespace: scenario-test
  name: limited-sa-pod-reader-binding
subjects:
- kind: ServiceAccount
  name: limited-sa
  namespace: scenario-test
roleRef:
  kind: Role
  name: pod-reader
  apiGroup: rbac.authorization.k8s.io
```
